# ECONOMICS.md

Assumptions and rough unit economics for the AI Spend Audit as a Credex lead-gen tool.

## Assumptions (placeholders; refine with real data)
- Average “high-savings” audit (> $500/mo savings): 5% of completed audits
- Lead capture rate after results: 8%
- Consult booking rate from high-savings audits: 15%
- Close rate from consult to credit purchase: 25%

## What is a converted lead worth?
Placeholder calculation (to be replaced with Credex real margins):
- Assume average first purchase: $5,000 of credits
- Assume gross margin: 15%
- Expected gross profit per purchase: $750

Expected value per completed audit:
- 1 audit → 0.05 high-savings
- 0.05 → 0.15 consult bookings = 0.0075
- 0.0075 → 0.25 closes = 0.001875 purchases
- 0.001875 × $750 ≈ $1.41 expected gross profit per audit

## CAC by channel (qualitative)
- Organic sharing (report URL): near $0, time cost only
- Founder DMs: time cost, but high intent
- HN/Reddit launches: bursty but cheap

## What conversion rates make this profitable?
If marginal platform costs (LLM summary + infra) average $0.10 per audit:
- Need expected gross profit per audit > $0.10
- With the above placeholders (~$1.41), the unit economics work.

## $1M ARR in 18 months (rough path)
If gross margin stays ~15%, $1M ARR needs ~$6.7M annual credit sales.
That implies:
- ~$560k credit sales / month
- If avg purchase is $5k, need ~112 purchases/month
- Backing out at 0.001875 purchases/audit → ~59,733 audits/month

This is a high bar and requires viral sharing + strong SEO + distribution partnerships.
