# GTM.md

## Target user
Early-stage startup founders and small-team engineering leads (2–50 people) who:
- Pay for multiple AI coding/writing tools monthly
- Don’t have a clean benchmark for “reasonable spend per developer”
- Feel cost creep but haven’t audited subscriptions vs API usage

## What they’re doing right before they want this tool
- Reviewing monthly burn in a spreadsheet
- Seeing renewals on a corporate card
- Debating “do we keep paying for both Claude and ChatGPT?”
- Reading posts about AI agent costs and usage-based billing changes

## Where they hang out (specific)
- Hacker News (Show HN, “Ask HN: tooling” threads)
- r/startups, r/programming, r/ExperiencedDevs
- Indie Hackers / small startup Slacks
- X: founder/operator lists, AI tooling builders, devtool newsletters

## First 100 users in 30 days ($0 paid)
1. Ship a shareable report URL with strong OG preview.
2. Post 3 “real audits” (sanitized) with before/after savings screenshots on X + HN “Show HN”.
3. DM founders who publicly discuss AI/tooling spend (“Cursor vs Copilot”, “Claude Code billing”, etc.) with a single screenshot + link.
4. Ask 10 devtool founders to share the tool as a benchmark reference (“what does your AI stack cost?”).

## Unfair channel
Credex’s discounted credits inventory: publish benchmark “street prices” and savings examples that others can’t match, then route high-savings users to Credex consult.

## Week-1 traction definition
- 100 completed audits
- 20 shared public reports
- 10 inbound “talk to Credex” clicks
- 5 lead captures from audits with >$500/mo savings
